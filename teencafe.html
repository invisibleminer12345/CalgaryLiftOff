TeenCafe

A welcoming community space designed for youth.

TeenCafe brings young people together for creative workshops, peer-led activities, and positive social connection.

What TeenCafe offers

  • Community events and social meetups
  • Skill-building workshops in arts, technology, and leadership
  • Peer mentorship and supportive spaces for youth voices

TeenCafe is open to youth looking for a welcoming environment to learn, collaborate, and grow.
