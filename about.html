About LiftOff!

Building a community where every person can thrive.

LiftOff! is one of the programs run by the Calgary Tsung Tsin Benevolent Association, a charity founded on the basis of connecting cultures around the city. Subsequently, membership to LiftOff! also includes membership to all other programs run by the Calgary Tsuin Tsin Benevolent Association. This program, also called the youth connection program, is completely youth-ran. From volunteering to leadership, LiftOff! provides opportunities for youth to make an impact improving diversity in the next generation.

Our Mission

LiftOff! was founded on the basis of providing unbiased opportunity to everyone, regardless of their background. We firmly believe that this can be accomplished through modern technology. Through the endless opportunities and experiences it creates, technology allows users to bond through shared experiences in a low-pressure environment. Unfortunately, not everyone has access to this luxury. Thus, the Cafe’s mission is to provide a public space for all youth to easily network with others.

Our Vision

A future where every emerging leader has access to guidance, community, and the tools needed to bring positive change to their neighborhoods and industries.

Core Values

  • Community-centered support
  • Friendly and welcoming environments
  • Opportunity for growth
  • Empowerment and leadership development

LiftOff! Empowering community growth through connection and action.
